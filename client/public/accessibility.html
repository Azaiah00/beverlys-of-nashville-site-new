Our commitment

Access for
every visitor.

Last reviewed August 8, 2026

Beverly's of Nashville wants its website and salon information to be usable by as many people as possible, including visitors who use keyboards, screen readers, magnification, voice control, captions, or reduced-motion settings.

What we are doing

  • Providing keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive headings, labels, and alternative text.
  • Supporting browser zoom and responsive layouts across desktop and mobile screens.
  • Respecting reduced-motion preferences and providing controls for video.
  • Using text contrast, meaningful link labels, and forms that can be completed with assistive technology.
  • Reviewing new pages and important updates for accessibility as the website develops.

Need information another way?

If a page, form, document, video, or portal feature is difficult to use, please tell us what you were trying to access and the assistive technology or browser you were using, if you are comfortable sharing it. We will make a reasonable effort to provide the information or service another way.
