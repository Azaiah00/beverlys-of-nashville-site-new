Teddy Chisom is a Nashville hairstylist, master colorist, and custom wig artist known online as The Hair Care King.

At Beverly's of Nashville, every appointment is personal. Teddy studies the condition, movement, texture, and lifestyle behind the look—then builds color, shape, or a custom unit around the person wearing it.
